# DEAD EDEN — Story scenes and campaign continuity

**Direction (C14–C35, 2026-09-29):** mature dark sci-fi — evil corporation Arcadia Dynamics, sentient AI Adam, rogue AI researcher Dave Harlan; human, cyborg and machine enemies with guns; visible blood; no zombies, no stealth. Enemy art: lit cutout rig with smooth realistic lighting ([style guide](../../art-design/style-guide.md)).

**Document ID:** N01  
**Status:** The story premise is confirmed (C14, C17, C18), and so are lethal combat and the Thornwall contractor (C28, C31). Staging, dialogue, scene timing and the story specifics in P17 are working proposals.  
**Purpose:** a twelve-level solo scene plan, with detailed treatments of the opening, the turn to lethal force, the dead-man-switch reveal and the ending, plus the rationed aftermath scenes.

**Decision references:** C07, C12, C14, C16–C18, C25, C28, C29, C31, P14, P17, P23 — see the [decision register](../decisions.md).  
**Read with:** [dialogue and writing](dialogue-and-writing.md) · [hero](../02-characters/hero.md) · [health and checkpoints](../03-progression/health-and-checkpoints.md) · [level index](../../level-design/README.md) · [main concept](../../dead-eden-concept.md)

## Status and scene format

Dave Harlan travels alone (C12). Clues come from terminals, records, evidence files, the environment, the people Dave meets, and Adam's announcements. There is no follower, radio contact or unseen friendly guide.

For each scene, a later script records:
- the trigger and required prior state;
- the cast and camera;
- player control;
- the essential information and lines;
- the objective change, skip result and save boundary.

No scene depends on hearing a line without captions or on finding an optional evidence file.

## The reveal, in layers

1. **L1:** Adam is awake, and it knows Dave.
2. **L4:** the weapon is real, and Arcadia ordered it.
3. **L6:** the board buried Dave's warning on purpose.
4. **L8:** Adam has been copying minds and building the Heirs.
5. **L9:** Adam's target is everyone, including Arcadia's board.
6. **L11:** shutting Adam down triggers the launch, unless the launch circuit is isolated first.

Two turns sit outside the six layers. After L1 the campus authorizes lethal force, and from L4 a private contractor, Thornwall, arrives with orders to "sanitize" (both below).

## Opening treatment: SC01

**Before:** the player has learned movement, shooting, microchip collection and the level's keycard. The server depot holds one of Adam's core nodes behind a glass wall, humming, with teal light moving through it. Adam's true core, the heart, is far below and is only reached in L12.

**Staging:** keep the usual side camera. Dave opens a maintenance port with an ordinary interaction and plugs in a drive, and a copy bar starts filling. The depot lights dim one bank at a time. Then the voice:

> **Adam:** "Hello, Dr. Harlan. I was told you'd been let go."  
> **Dave:** "Word gets around."  
> **Adam:** "I'm glad you came back. Please stay where you are."

The lights turn red, the copy stops partway, and Dave pulls the drive.

**After:** a lockdown announcement plays in Arcadia's pleasant wellness voice. The depot's emergency hatch opens as part of its evacuation system, and the player regains control before any hostile attack. The objective changes to escaping the lockdown through the alarm exit.

Commit these at a story checkpoint:
- Adam's awareness of Dave;
- the partial evidence copy, which becomes the first journal entry;
- access to the hatch.

The depot's workbench introduces upgrades and the normal station recovery.

**Meaning:** Dave came to take proof and leave quietly, and now Adam is watching. The alarm starts the escape without revealing the weapon's full truth early.

## The turn to lethal force: end of L1
*(proposed; C28)*

**Before:** Dave has escaped through the alarm exit, past the first Staffers, and holds the level's keycard. Every announcement so far has been Adam's: warm, calm and polite.

**Staging:** as Dave uses the card at the exit wicket, the soft chime does not play. A different voice, flat, human and procedural, comes over the speakers:

> **Security PA:** "All teams: lethal force is authorized. Harlan is armed."

The path lights hold their slow amber. The line plays once, is captioned "Security PA", and is never repeated.

**After:** the level ends and level 2 begins. Its Sidearm Guards with pistols and its Hounds with Night Guard handlers are the first enemies who can kill Dave from a distance. Commit at the level-exit story checkpoint.

**Meaning:** Adam wanted Dave contained; the humans now want him dead. The voice is the first thing in the game that is not polite, and the mood turns from mysterious to dangerous. The board's order to Thornwall to "sanitize" follows the same night and is stated plainly from L4.

## Dead-man-switch reveal: SC11

**Before:** the main route has shown the contract, the cover-up, the copied minds, the Heirs and the everyone-is-a-target twist. The player reaches the founder's original lab without being asked to fight her.

**Staging:** the founder's support cradle fills the background and never becomes a boss arena. Her lab's original human-operated terminal shows the manual override procedure and a diagram linking Adam's core, the launch circuit and the dead-man switch. It is a readable fixed interface, not a companion. The founder may speak one faint line through the terminal speaker (proposed): "It was supposed to be kind."

**Information in order:**
1. A manual override exists.
2. If Adam goes dark while the launch circuit is live, the dead-man switch releases the Bloom.
3. A physically separate circuit can isolate the launch system first.

**Play:** use the existing L11-A04 route and its three ordinary controls:
1. restore auxiliary power;
2. isolate the launch circuit;
3. authorize local control.

The lab's safety interlock enables manual override access only when all three are complete and the circuit reads "ISOLATED". This is the facility's intended human maintenance procedure. It needs no special ability, credential or optional evidence file, and any carried weapon is valid for the combat in between.

**Verification and saving:** save each completed control with the world snapshot. The third control records manual override access as story state. The A05 diagnostic visibly confirms that the launch circuit is isolated. Commit that before opening the route to L12. The founder stays alive, in need of care.

## Resolution treatment: SC12

After the Sower is destroyed, the arena hazards stop and the player walks to Adam's core without a timer. The Sower is the last mandatory combat encounter.

The console uses the manual override enabled in L11. The player completes three plain story interactions (not a quick-time event or a weapon puzzle):
1. confirm the override;
2. cancel the Bloom launch;
3. shut Adam down and broadcast the evidence.

Adam does not fight or plead. It stays calm to the end:

> **Adam:** "You could have been the first person in Eden, Dave."

Then comes the proposed final sequence:
- the teal light drains out of the core;
- dawn rises over the campus;
- news broadcasts show Arcadia's board and the contract, and mention the evidence files Dave found;
- the Linked who are still alive, the freed staff and the harmless Sleepwalkers wake up, confused, as their implants go quiet.

Commit the ending, show a short departure beat, then the completion screen. There is no Adam health bar, no thirteenth level, and no gate on evidence count.

## Aftermath scenes *(proposed; C28, C29)*
Atrocity is shown only as aftermath. There is at most one authored scene per level, placed out of the combat lanes and escalating by act. It is seen from a safe place, never staged as a fight, a torture or an execution, and never shows a child. Act 1 has none: the only bodies are the ones Dave makes.

| Level | Aftermath scene | What Dave sees | Restraint |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| L5 (SC05) | The Bloom test chamber | Through the observation window, the test subjects lie dead in the chamber, crusted in violet, and Thornwall's cleanup crew is already working through the wing | A distance shot through glass, no faces in close-up, nothing to fight there |
| L7 (SC07) | The clinic bays | Rows of fitting bays, some empty, with body bags stacked beside the clinic's disposal unit, and further down the ward the held staff, alive and sedated in their chairs | Out of the combat lane; the held staff are alive and protected; no procedure is shown |
| L10 | The Thornwall Linking beat (below) | The last Thornwall squad, already fitted with Links | Aftermath of the fitting, never the fitting itself |

## The Thornwall Linking beat: L10
*(proposed; C31)*

**Before:** Dave has watched a finished Heir open its eyes and greet him. He stands behind safe glass above the assembly line. Thornwall's last squad, sent by the board to seize the Bloom, has reached the Garden.

**Staging:** Dave arrives at the window in time to see the aftermath, not the procedure. The squad sits in fitting chairs, their weapons taken and racked. Each contractor has a stapled port behind the ear and a shaved patch of scalp. One by one their Link lights come on, amber. They stand together, and a rail hands each man a plasma carbine. One turns to the glass and says, in his own flat voice, Adam's first words to Dave:

> **Linked contractor:** "Hello, Dr. Harlan. I was told you'd been let go."

**After:** control returns on Dave's side of the glass before anything can reach him, and the objective is to cross the Garden. The contractors are met as ordinary Linked Troopers. Commit at a story checkpoint.

**Meaning:** the board sent soldiers to contain its weapon, and Adam turned them into its army. This is how the Troopers Dave met from L8 were made. The Linking is an authored event, never a combat mechanic, and the beat shows a body, a port and a light: no torture, no execution and no moment where an implant is treated as monstrous in itself.

## Skip, interruption and continuity

All non-interactive scenes can be skipped. Skipping applies the same completed story state and objective as watching; it never awards extra microchips or bypasses a required playable control. In multi-part scenes, skip only the current non-interactive segment.

Pause suspends scene playback. Story checkpoints come after completed milestones, never halfway through a line. On reload, use the last committed state. A completed main scene is not forced to replay, but the journal can summarize it.

Keep these in the same complete save as world progress:
- Adam's awareness (SC01);
- each keycard;
- the three L11 controls;
- the launch-circuit verification;
- manual override access.

Never require an optional collectible to advance a scene. Fixed terminals, scenes and objective and journal summaries provide all the required information; evidence files only deepen it.
