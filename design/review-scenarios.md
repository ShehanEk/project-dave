# Design consistency review scenarios

**Direction (C14–C35, 2026-09-29):** mature dark sci-fi — evil corporation Arcadia Dynamics, sentient AI Adam, rogue AI researcher Dave Harlan; human, cyborg and machine enemies with guns; visible blood; no zombies, no stealth. Enemy art: lit cutout rig with smooth realistic lighting ([style guide](../art-design/style-guide.md)).

Use these cases after changing related documents. They are **written acceptance scenarios**, not automated tests, prototype results, or proof of balanced gameplay. Review the proposed baseline as a whole; when deliberately changing a proposal, update its expected outcomes here. RV28–RV39 are *proposed* additions that cover keycards, lockdown events, dark-scene options, lethal combat and blood, and the enemy gun kit.

## Visual direction review

When generating any asset or scene, use the lit cutout rig for enemies (C35: painted parts in flat base colors with clean outlines, no baked lighting or shadows, matching normal maps and engine lighting), C15's palette and mood, and the shared [style guide](../art-design/style-guide.md). Verify linework, flat color masses, stable part counts and readable side poses, and check every design against a dark background under the engine lamps and each gun's muzzle flash. Review asymmetric details separately for each facing direction. A scene's drawn depth must not add an off-plane gameplay route. The look is confirmed direction with a validated by the approved lit-cutout test (2026-09-30), so every enemy asset remains a proposal until the user approves it. Dave's placeholder sprites are kept and get normal maps. The old zombie art and daytime Eon City scenes were deleted (C23), and the concept art of the removed first-level machine was deleted with it (C32).

## Repository checks

Every design document should have a unique ID, explicit proposal status, valid local links, decision references, and an index entry. The manifest should resolve every document and task route. The campaign remains twelve levels with six ordered main-route areas each; art coverage is 24 enemy types (four Arcadia Security, three Thornwall, four Linked, two dogs, nine machines and two Heirs), one protected NPC, four mini-bosses, nine enemy guns, and five weapons.

No duplicate ZIP archive, hidden extra weapon, obsolete two-weapon combo, free tether, stealth or detection system, takeover or conversion mechanic, or contradictory upgrade ownership rule should be introduced. No pre-revamp story terms should remain: the AI is Adam, the hero is Dave Harlan, and the collectibles are microchips and evidence files. No zombies, removed enemy types or pre-C28 rules (cyborgs that cannot die, a ban on blood) should appear as current text, and the renamed levels (Curfew, Test Subjects, Cold Storage) and upgrades (Extended Tube, Incendiary Shells, Twin Shell, Snare Foam, Cluster Charges) should be used everywhere. Check names and upgrade capabilities against their existing art briefs.

## What this does not establish

A written scenario can reveal an inconsistent rule, but cannot demonstrate fun, readable timing, reachable geometry, controller feel, frame performance, accessibility effectiveness, or economy balance. Those require later prototypes or asset reviews after the user requests implementation.
