# Enemy interactions and environmental objects

**Direction (C14–C35, 2026-09-29):** mature dark sci-fi — evil corporation Arcadia Dynamics, sentient AI Adam, rogue AI researcher Dave Harlan; human, cyborg and machine enemies with guns; visible blood; no zombies, no stealth. Enemy art: lit cutout rig with smooth realistic lighting ([style guide](../../art-design/style-guide.md)).

Combine Arcadia Security, Thornwall, Adam's machines, the Linked, the cyborg dogs, the Heirs and Arcadia's facilities into predictable interactions and fair single-weapon encounters, in scenes where darkness never hides gameplay.

This is section 04 of the concept design pack. Read the [decision register](../decisions.md) before treating a proposal as fixed.
