# Rook (H01) — 2D identity prompt

> **Record (C35):** this is the generation record of the placeholder Rook sprite pack in [sprites-v1](sprites-v1/), which now stands in for Dave Harlan. It describes the old hero Rook and the pre-revamp look (cel shadows, stylized proportions) and is kept for history. New character art follows the lit cutout pipeline in the [style guide](../../art-design/style-guide.md) (C35): flat, evenly lit paintings with a normal map for each part or frame.

**Visual direction when the pack was made (C11):** [Hand-drawn 2D](../../art-design/style-guide.md) — clean outlines, flat colors, cel shadows and layered scenery. [Selected references](../README.md).

**Status:** Generation record. The sprites in [sprites-v1](sprites-v1/) were made from these prompts. Dave's current look is the proposed [H01 hero brief](../../design/02-characters/hero.md).

## 1. Identity reference

```text
Style: confident dark olive or warm charcoal outlines, heavier outer contour, restrained interior lines, broad flat local colors, one or two crisp cel-shadow shapes and sparse graphic highlights.
Draw finished hand-drawn 2D game concept art of an original protagonist for a colorful side-scrolling platformer shooter: Dave Harlan, an adult practical scavenger, a man in his late twenties, compact build, about 1.70 m, roughly five-and-a-half heads tall, strong hands and broad practical boots. Warm brown skin, cropped dark hair with one uneven forelock, expressive brows, a small healed mark through one eyebrow, quietly resourceful expression with a hint of dry humor.
Outfit: rust-orange short work jacket over a dark teal shirt, short cream neck cloth, cream reinforced trousers with charcoal knee patches, broad scuffed brown boots, one small belt pouch. Short straps only.
Pose: full body, three-quarter view turned toward the viewer's right, relaxed ready stance, both hands empty. Keep the side silhouette readable for a side-scrolling game.
One complete subject on a flat warm off-white background with a simple flat contact shadow. No weapons, spare guns or holsters, no armor suit, cape, helmet, backpack arsenal or recognizable franchise costume. No realistic surface shading, studio gradients, volumetric light, text, labels, UI or watermark.
```

Save the chosen result as `h01-rook-2d-v1.png` in this folder, then record the choice in a `SELECTED.md`.
