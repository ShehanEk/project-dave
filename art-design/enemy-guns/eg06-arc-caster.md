# "Groundline" Arc Caster

**Direction (C14–C35, 2026-09-29):** mature dark sci-fi — evil corporation Arcadia Dynamics, sentient AI Adam, rogue AI researcher Dave Harlan; human, cyborg and machine enemies with guns; visible blood; no zombies, no stealth. Enemy art: lit cutout rig with smooth realistic lighting ([style guide](../style-guide.md)).

**Asset ID:** EG06\
**Category:** enemy-guns\
**First appearance:** Level 6\
**Design status:** Enemy art direction (C35): a lit cutout rig with smooth, realistic engine lighting; confirmed direction, validated by the approved lit-cutout test (2026-09-30). This gun's name, look, colors and numbers are *proposed* (C27, P23), and no image is approved yet. Dimensions are provisional art proportions, not engine specifications.

## Identity and carriers

**Maker:** Arcadia Dynamics. The "Groundline" is Rootworks power-line hardware, a grounding rod that Adam turns on people. It is a futuristic gun.

**Carried by:** [Linked Linemen](../linked/lk02-linked-lineman.md), L6–L7; [Howard Stroud](../mini-bosses/b02-howard-stroud.md), L6, as a striker head on his right cable arm; and [the Sower](../mini-bosses/b04-the-sower.md)'s stomp, L12, as striker plates on its feet. It drops on death as a static prop that Dave cannot pick up (Stroud's and the Sower's strikers are part of their bodies).

It teaches the floor arc: jump it, or stand on another platform.

## Look, size and socket

**Futuristic look:** a heavy insulated rod with a ceramic striker tip, wired to a back capacitor pack. On the Lineman the cables are sutured into his forearms. The rod is dark rubber-insulated steel with pale ceramic bands, ending in a pale ceramic spike with a dark metal collar, and it trails its own short cable bundle from the rear grip to a plug socket on the carrier's pack.

**Size:** the rod is 1.25 m long and the striker tip 0.14 m, about two-thirds of the Lineman's height. The back capacitor pack belongs to each carrier's own rig (the Lineman's is a boxy gunmetal pack with a ceramic-capped terminal; Stroud's is a rack of three capacitors). Stroud's striker head is the same design at about 1.6 times scale, and the Sower's striker plates are the same design at about 3 times scale.

**Socket:** the grip origin sits at the rear third of the rod, where the weapon hand's palm socket meets it, with the support hand forward (drawn on the carrier's hand). The rod points down when it strikes. The arc origin marker sits at the striker tip. On Stroud the origin is his right wrist socket; on the Sower it is the center of each sole plate.

**Colors (flat, unlit):** steel #1C2A3A rod with rubber insulation; pale ceramic #DDE5EA bands and striker; small teal #3FE0D0 status lights, steady; a short black cable bundle.

## Fire pattern, tell and dodge

**Fire:** a crackling arc 0.4 H tall crawls along the floor at 5 H/s. It dies at a ledge, a wall or 6.5 H. 1 damage. Then a 1.4 s recharge, rooted. Stroud and the Sower send an arc each way.

**Tell (CHARGE):** the capacitor pack glow grows amber, then red for the last 0.25 s, with a rising buzz, and then the rod is rammed into the deck.

**Dodge:** jump it (it crosses Dave in about 0.2 s), or stand on another platform, since the arc cannot leave its floor.

## Shot, light and sound

**Projectile:** a jagged blue-white line with a white core and a #5AA9FF edge, re-jagged at 12 Hz (held static under reduced motion), plus sparks. It has its own shape: a crawling, jagged ground line, never a ball or a straight bolt. Shots are never gold, violet, amber, red, teal or green.

**Muzzle-flash light (*proposed* preset):** a soft round blue-white light (white tinted with the energy edge), #DCEBFF, energy 1.4, extent 1.8 H at the strike, height 0.2 H, one 0.12 s pulse. The crawling arc carries a small traveling light, energy 0.7, extent 1.0 H and height 0.15 H, that follows its head along the floor. Both light the carrier and the deck through their normal maps.

**Sound:** a rising buzz during the charge, the strike, then a snap when the arc dies.

## Rules and production

One rod and one striker tip; no brass, gold or legible text, and maker marks are plain stripes. The capacitor pack and lights are dark or small and steady in the base art, and the arc, sparks and glow are effects, never painted onto the rod. Energy blue stays out of the tell: amber and red are the only tell colors. Sutured cables are shown as clean hardware ports, never monstrous or bloody. Cut the rod as one part with its own normal map (green = up). Approve the neutral image before the carried study, and check the rod at gameplay size in a Lineman's hands, on Stroud's arm and as the Sower's sole plates, in grayscale and against near-black backgrounds. The lit cutout look is a confirmed direction, validated by the approved lit-cutout test (2026-09-30) (C35).
