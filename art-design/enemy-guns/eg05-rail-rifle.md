# RX-2 "Needle" Rail Rifle

## Identity and carriers

**Maker:** Arcadia Dynamics' defense division. The RX-2 "Needle" is an Arcadia prototype that Thornwall field-tests on Dave, so it wears Arcadia's clean white-and-graphite body with Thornwall's field tape on the grip. It is a futuristic gun.

**Carried by:** Thornwall [Marksmen](../thornwall/tw03-marksman.md) on far perches, L6–L9. Any enemy can carry it. It drops on death as a static prop that Dave cannot pick up. At most one is on screen.

It teaches the sight line: keep moving, and advance one piece of cover per recharge.

## Look, size and socket

**Futuristic look:** a long flat twin-rail barrel with three capacitor rings that light up in turn, in a graphite and white body with a folding stock. The rings are dark ceramic bands along the barrel, unlit in the base art. The twin rails are two thin steel bars with a pale gap between them.

**Size:** 1.35 m long and 0.26 m tall with the rings, about three-quarters of an adult carrier's height and the longest hand-held gun in the kit. The stock is drawn extended.

**Socket:** the grip origin sits at the pistol grip, where the weapon hand's palm socket meets it; the rifle rotates about this point to aim, shouldered. The muzzle marker sits at the front of the rail gap, where the sight line and the shot start. The support-hand point sits under the rail cradle (drawn on the carrier's support hand).

**Colors (flat, unlit):** graphite #2A3341 body; Arcadia white #D5DDE3 side panels and stock; steel #1C2A3A rails; dark ceramic #2E3B4E capacitor rings; black cloth tape on the grip.

## Fire pattern, tell and dodge

**Fire:** one instant slug to the first wall or to Dave: 2 damage, full-screen range. Then a 2.2 s recharge, rooted.

**Tell (LINE):** a 1.2 s sight line tracks Dave's grounded point at 3 H/s while the three rings light 1-2-3 in turn (cold white). Then the line freezes and holds amber for 0.3 s and turns red for the last 0.25 s. The sight line is a thin pale line from the muzzle to its aim point, with a dark edge so it reads on any floor; it changes color at the hold and at the red, and the rings change with it.

**Dodge:** keep moving; a moving Dave is usually off the line by the freeze. Jump a flat line. Lines are only flat or at 45 degrees or steeper. Advance one cover piece per recharge.

## Shot, light and sound

**Projectile:** a white-hot line afterimage, a white core with a #5AA9FF edge and a thin dark outline, fading over 0.15 s, plus a hit spark. It has its own shape: a straight thin line, never a bolt or a ball. Shots are never gold, violet, amber, red, teal or green.

**Muzzle-flash light (*proposed* preset):** a soft round blue-white light (white tinted with the energy edge), #DCEBFF, energy 2.0, extent 2.6 H, height 0.3 H, one 0.15 s fade to match the afterimage. It lights the carrier and the perch through their normal maps. The camera shakes only if Dave is hit, and never under reduced motion.

**Sound:** a 3-step rising whine as the rings light, then a hard crack with a ringing tail.

## Rules and production

Exactly three capacitor rings and two rails; no scope, brass, gold or legible text, and maker marks are plain stripes. The rings and lens are dark in the base art, and the sight line, glow and afterimage are effects, never painted onto the gun. Energy blue stays out of the tell: amber and red are the only tell colors. Cut the gun as one part with its own normal map (green = up), with each capacitor ring as its own light layer. Approve the neutral image before the carried study, and check the rifle at gameplay size on a far perch, in grayscale and against near-black backgrounds. The lit cutout look is a confirmed direction, validated by the approved lit-cutout test (2026-09-30) (C35).
