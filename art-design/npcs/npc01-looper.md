# Looper

**Direction (C14–C35, 2026-09-29; C56–C60, 2026-10-12):** mature corporate-cyberpunk sci-fi — evil corporation Arcadia Dynamics, sentient AI Adam, rogue AI researcher Dave Harlan; human, cyborg and machine enemies with guns; visible blood; no zombies, no stealth. ## Identity and role

Harmless protected NPC: a Linked worker whose failing implant loops an old work routine. Never a target, never hostile, no combat.

*Proposed identity (C60):* an awake member of Arcadia's Wellness Center facilities and records staff, stuck in a routine by a failing Link. Adam's control has frayed, and the body falls back on the routines of its old job, eyes open and busy: filing charts, wiping a desk, pushing a cart, walking the same stretch of corridor, and covering its head in an old shelter drill when shots go off nearby. Its port light flickers. It carries no weapon, makes no attack and has no hit zone: player shots and enemy shots pass through it, no hit spark or blood is drawn, and encounters place it out of fire lines. Protected people are untouchable.

**Three looks on one rig** (C56, C60), all Caucasian (C43): **A, the records clerk** (a woman in her forties, dark hair in a neat bun), **B, the porter** (a man in his thirties, short fair hair), **C, the archivist** (a man in his fifties, grey hair, thin glasses). It wears no neon trim, because electric blue marks people Adam is driving and nobody is driving a Looper. The game picks a look by entity id.

It appears in Levels 7–9 as quiet background life in the clinic corridors and the archive, and it is an unsettling sight: staff stuck in their work, busy and polite, doing the same few things again and again. It teaches that not every moving body is a target, and it never blocks progress.

About 1.70 m upright, roughly 7.25 heads, with a slim build.

It has no headgear, no hardware bulk and no held weapon, which sets it apart from every driven Linked person and from the electric-blue Linked types.

## Appearance and construction

*Proposed design (C60).* A neat knee-length service coat in charcoal-navy, belted at the waist, over a pale shirt, dark navy trousers and practical shoes. A lanyard holds a blank ID plate, and a small dull brass key ring hangs from the near side of the belt as a personal keepsake. A slim graphite bracer with a small status window, painted dark, sits on the near wrist. The face is awake and attentive: open, focused eyes that follow the task and blink normally, soft brows and a faint polite look. It wears no neon trim.

The Link port sits behind the near ear: a clean coin-sized disc with a pale steel rim and a thin ring light, set flush in the skin, with no staples, bruise ring or seam. The port is painted dark and unlit; the engine adds a dim teal light that flickers. The hardware is small and neat, so the figure reads as a person. There are no logos or legible text.

## Abilities and movement

- **Template:** none: a protected NPC, not an enemy (the behavior templates are for enemies).
- **Weapon:** none. It has no attack, no tell and no hit zone.
- **Behavior:** it repeats old work routines on a fixed loop, awake and busy.
- **Reaction:** when shots or explosions go off nearby it flinches and covers its head, then resumes; it never runs into a fire line.
- **Counter:** none needed. It is never a target, encounters place it out of fire lines, and it never blocks progress.

Works through a fixed loop at a steady, purposeful pace: filing a chart, wiping a desk, pushing an empty cart along a fixed path, reaching for a door handle that is or isn't there, walking the same stretch of corridor. When the loop ends the routine snaps back to its first step: the body hitches for a beat while the port light flickers, then it starts again.

When shots or explosions go off nearby it crouches and covers its head in an old shelter drill, then rises and resumes. It may also stand at a wall console and tap it in a loop.

**Sample barks** *(proposed; the Looper's own murmured habits, not Adam's words. Each is subtitle text plus a non-verbal shout or grunt sound, since there is no voice pipeline)*:

- "Just a moment."
- "Badge, please."
- "Meeting's at nine."
- "Filing that now."

It never shouts, and it has no death sound, since it cannot be hurt.

## Protection and limitations

There is no opening, because it is not a target: it has no hit zone, and shots pass through it. Keep it visibly harmless: a relaxed, busy posture and a dim teal light that flickers but is never amber or red. Do not give it a weapon, a hostile glow or a monstrous change.
