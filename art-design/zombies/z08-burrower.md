# Burrower

**Approved visual direction (C11):** [Hand-drawn 2D](../style-guide.md) — clean outlines, flat colors, cel shadows and layered scenery. [Selected references](../../concept-art/README.md).

**Asset ID:** Z08\
**Category:** zombies\
**First appearance:** Level 5\
**Design status:** Confirmed hand-drawn 2D rendering style (C11); this asset's unselected appearance details remain proposed. Dimensions are provisional art proportions, not engine specifications.

## Identity and role

Subsurface ambusher from the soil-treatment workforce.

## Scale and silhouette

1.25 m crouched; 1.60 m if upright; about 0.95 m across oversized forearms.

A low wedge-shaped body with massive forearms, broad shovel-like gloved hands, small tucked legs, and a head shielded by a work hood.

Use a neutral 1.70 m human silhouette as a temporary scale reference on a separate comparison sheet. The hero's final design is not fixed by this measurement.

## Appearance and construction

The dusty tan hood frames a simple human face and cracked goggles. Two forearms are swollen beneath reinforced soil-worker gloves. Each glove has four broad padded finger lobes plus a separate thumb; the shape suggests a shovel without becoming a metal drill. A moss-colored utility vest and short kneepads complete the outfit.

## Color and materials

Clay-brown skin #AD9477; tan hood #C9B28A; moss vest #758268; dark glove tips #625B4B; cream goggle rims #DDD0B1.

Color values are palette targets for later material work; image generators may approximate them. Pair important cues with geometry, posture, and contrast so they do not depend on color alone.

## Abilities and movement

Travels beneath soft soil and attacks the hero's last marked position. Cannot burrow through every surface.

Digs with alternating arm sweeps, vanishes into soft ground, and leaves a traveling soil ripple. A mound swells before it erupts, lands with both hands wide, and shakes dirt from its face.

## Openings and limitations

The emergence and dirt-shaking recovery expose the whole body. Surface ripples are part of the warning system, not modeled onto its body.

## Parts to keep separate for animation

Body; enlarged forearms and hands; hood; goggles; vest; kneepads; separate soil mound, ripple, and dust effects.

Plan overlapping drawing layers and visible pivots for the intended animation method. Keep projectiles, attack trails, warning overlays, impacts and environmental props separate from the character or weapon. These are illustrated components, not a mandated rig: frame-by-frame, cutout or hybrid animation remains a later production choice.

## Required pose and state references

Crouched surface idle; digging; subterranean ripple-only effect; eruption; landing; recovery.

Make these as separate studies after the neutral design is approved. Use the approved neutral image as a reference so poses do not silently change anatomy or equipment.

## Consistency rules

Two arms and two legs; human-derived hands. No worm tail, drill machinery, robot shell, or permanent dirt cloud hiding the silhouette.

Anatomical left and right refer to the subject's own sides, not the viewer's. Do not automatically mirror an asymmetrical design when generating the opposite view.
