# Graftback

**Asset ID:** Z09\
**Category:** zombies\
**First appearance:** Level 6\
**Design status:** Proposed visual direction for the established concept. Dimensions are provisional art proportions, not engine specifications.

## Identity and role

Heavy transplant patient with protected front and exposed back.

## Scale and silhouette

2.45 m tall, 1.65 m across shoulders; hands reach below the knees.

A huge rounded trapezoid of shoulders and chest over short legs, with two long heavy arms. Its front reads as layered closed tissue plates; its back contains a distinct recessed patch.

Use a neutral 1.70 m human silhouette as a temporary scale reference on a separate comparison sheet. The hero's final design is not fixed by this measurement.

## Appearance and construction

Overlapping scar-tissue folds form broad rounded bands across the chest and forearms, not metal armor. Remnants of a dark treatment harness loop over the shoulders and around the waist. A salmon-colored patch of thin intact tissue sits high between the shoulder blades, bounded by a clear oval seam. Small tired eyes and a blunt nose preserve a human presence.

## Color and materials

Desaturated stone skin #A7AA94; muted mauve tissue layers #948395; salmon back patch #D18E84; faded navy harness #4E6070; cream wraps #D5C8AB.

Color values are palette targets for later material work; image generators may approximate them. Pair important cues with geometry, posture, and contrast so they do not depend on color alone.

## Abilities and movement

Absorbs frontal punishment with thick tissue and delivers a heavy ground slam. Its size prevents tether capture.

Moves with slow weight shifts. Before a slam, both arms rise and the torso stretches, then the fists land and the body remains hunched forward, exposing the back.

## Openings and limitations

Thin back patch during slam recovery. Distinguish protected and vulnerable tissue through thickness, roughness, and silhouette, not color alone.

## Parts to keep separate for modeling

Large humanoid body; layered front forms; back patch material region; harness; arm and shoulder deformation areas; separate ground-impact effect.

Preserve articulation clearance in the neutral pose. Keep projectiles, attack trails, glow cards, impact effects, and environmental props separate from the core model. A part list describes visual assembly, not manufacturing internals.

## Required pose and state references

Guarded approach; two-arm lift; slam; bent recovery with back exposed; stagger; defeated kneel.

Make these as separate studies after the neutral design is approved. Use the approved neutral image as a reference so poses do not silently change anatomy or equipment.

## Consistency rules

Exactly two arms and two legs. Extra transplanted tissue must not become extra hands or faces. No metal chest armor or infected robot components.

Anatomical left and right refer to the subject's own sides, not the viewer's. Do not automatically mirror an asymmetrical design when generating the opposite view.
