# Gardener

**Approved visual direction (C11):** [Hand-drawn 2D](../style-guide.md) — clean outlines, flat colors, cel shadows and layered scenery. [Selected references](../../concept-art/README.md).

**Asset ID:** Z03\
**Category:** zombies\
**First appearance:** Level 5\
**Design status:** Confirmed hand-drawn 2D rendering style (C11); this asset's unselected appearance details remain proposed. Dimensions are provisional art proportions, not engine specifications.

## Identity and role

Rooted area-control zombie shaped by botanical treatment.

## Scale and silhouette

1.85 m tall including shoulder growth; spread roots occupy about 1.40 m.

A hunched human gardener with two thick root-wrapped boots, broad glove-like hands, and an asymmetrical cluster of three leaf shoots over the anatomical left shoulder.

Use a neutral 1.70 m human silhouette as a temporary scale reference on a separate comparison sheet. The hero's final design is not fixed by this measurement.

## Appearance and construction

A mustard work apron and muted green shirt remain visible under coarse woody bands. Roots follow the outside of the forearms and calves rather than replacing the entire anatomy. A tilted broad-brim hat has one missing wedge. The feet can unfold four thick anchoring roots in total. The lower abdomen has a rounded woody knot with a closed seam.

## Color and materials

Moss skin #8A9B72; bark brown #786348; mustard apron #C6A34C; muted leaf green #658E52; ochre shoot tips #E0BE67.

Color values are palette targets for later material work; image generators may approximate them. Pair important cues with geometry, posture, and contrast so they do not depend on color alone.

## Abilities and movement

Anchors itself and creates ground-level thorn growth. The hero can destroy exposed growth or move to another platform.

Shuffles heavily, plants both feet, lowers its hands, and sends a visible pulse down the roots before a line of thorns rises along the floor. It pauses while pulling its roots free.

## Openings and limitations

Exposed roots and thorn sprouts are interruptible targets. It remains a biological patient rather than a robot controlling plants.

## Parts to keep separate for animation

Humanoid body; clothing; hat; three shoulder shoots; four deployable foot roots; separate floor-thorn segments and traveling ground effect.

Plan overlapping drawing layers and visible pivots for the intended animation method. Keep projectiles, attack trails, warning overlays, impacts and environmental props separate from the character or weapon. These are illustrated components, not a mandated rig: frame-by-frame, cutout or hybrid animation remains a later production choice.

## Required pose and state references

Walking roots tucked; anchoring; ground pulse tell; thorn attack; uprooting recovery; damaged roots; defeated.

Make these as separate studies after the neutral design is approved. Use the approved neutral image as a reference so poses do not silently change anatomy or equipment.

## Consistency rules

Two human arms and two legs remain readable. Exactly three large shoulder shoots, four main foot roots. No flower turret barrel or autonomous robot parts.

Anatomical left and right refer to the subject's own sides, not the viewer's. Do not automatically mirror an asymmetrical design when generating the opposite view.
