# Sanitizer

**Asset ID:** R08\
**Category:** robots\
**First appearance:** Level 7\
**Design status:** Proposed visual direction for the established concept. Dimensions are provisional art proportions, not engine specifications.

## Identity and role

Short-range heat sweeper that controls sections of the floor.

## Scale and silhouette

1.45 m tall; 1.00 m wide with side treads.

A compact barrel-shaped machine on two short tank treads. A large rounded tank rises behind the body, and one thick front-mounted nozzle sits on a swiveling joint.

Use a neutral 1.70 m human silhouette as a temporary scale reference on a separate comparison sheet. The hero's final design is not fixed by this measurement.

## Appearance and construction

Use a cream pressure-vessel silhouette with three broad protective ribs and a visible nonnumeric heat gauge. The nozzle is a flattened bell with a thick ceramic lip. One protected hose loops visibly from tank to nozzle. Two side cooling fins hinge outward. A simple face panel sits above the nozzle.

## Color and materials

Off-white #E7E3D5; safety coral #DB715B; charcoal treads #333B43; cool teal gauge #60B6B4; hot amber internal light #FFAC48.

Color values are palette targets for later material work; image generators may approximate them. Pair important cues with geometry, posture, and contrast so they do not depend on color alone.

## Abilities and movement

Projects a short heat sweep that leaves temporary burning ground. Must stop to cool after sustained use.

Advances slowly, braces its treads, and sweeps the nozzle across a low arc. Before firing, the tank window fills with light and the nozzle tilts toward the ground. Cooling fins open as the machine pauses.

## Openings and limitations

The cooling pause allows safe attacks. Show the gauge, expanding fins, and slowing posture as readable cues; avoid suggesting the tank automatically explodes.

## Parts to keep separate for modeling

Body; two tread units; tank; nozzle and swivel; one thick hose; two hinged cooling fins; face panel. Ground fire is a separate effect.

Preserve articulation clearance in the neutral pose. Keep projectiles, attack trails, glow cards, impact effects, and environmental props separate from the core model. A part list describes visual assembly, not manufacturing internals.

## Required pose and state references

Patrol; heat warning; sweep left and right; hot ground effect separate; cooling fins open; disabled.

Make these as separate studies after the neutral design is approved. Use the approved neutral image as a reference so poses do not silently change anatomy or equipment.

## Consistency rules

No human arms or legs, no flesh, one nozzle and one protected hose. Keep the nozzle low enough to explain the floor sweep.

Anatomical left and right refer to the subject's own sides, not the viewer's. Do not automatically mirror an asymmetrical design when generating the opposite view.
